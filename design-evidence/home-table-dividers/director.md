## Cycle 52 — REJECT

The Visual Design, Responsive and Accessibility, and CSS and Quartz V5 reviewers all requested revision. On the 900px Chrome first load, I reproduced the accepted table's missing 81px of the top rule, then reproduced new gaps on the candidate's first two internal boundaries. On the proposed build the boundary under Obtained loses ink under the pinned label and in the right-edge fade. Reject the proposed split borders and vertical restoration script. The build emitted 913 files and all 167 unit tests passed. ## Cycle 54 — REVISE

All three independent reviewers approved the compiled CSS on port 48445. The previous material defect is resolved. In my full-viewport Firefox replay at 1280 × 874, both themes draw the top edge beneath “Obtained” on initial render, through a native 45px wheel pan and refresh. Chrome and Brave drew all four rules across all 48 of my static, cold-initialized, native-pan and refresh captures at 390, 900 and 1440 CSS px in light and dark modes. Blocking webfonts and the postscript in Chrome preserved all four strokes before and after those resources loaded. [Firefox](director/director54-evidence/ff-48445-js-ink.json) · [Chrome and Brave](director/director54-evidence/measured.json) · [Early paint](director/director54-evidence/font-metrics-gaps.json)

A separate **material first-load regression** remains in Firefox. At 503 × 874 in light mode, the first three rules each covered all 443 sampled columns, while the last rule was absent across all 443. The adjacent native horizontal scrollbar begins immediately below the row. At 505 × 874 the same failure covers 445 columns. The accepted page draws the bottom line in both widths. I reproduced this using unmodified pages in separate launches. A narrow frame during a viewport change initially exposed the problem at 500px. The defect in the stable 503px and 505px geometries is independently repeatable. A wider sample of 20 widths found this regression at those two widths. As elsewhere, the horizontal direction and the settings of the scroll controller have no bearing on drawing the rule at initial offset zero.
