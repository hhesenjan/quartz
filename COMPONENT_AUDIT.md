# Quartz V5 component audit

The published corpus contains 252 HTML documents. Of these, 91 are alias redirects, 160 have the regular frame and one has the minimal 404 frame. The 160 comprise Home, 56 Blog articles, seven certification articles, 20 project articles, seven folder listings, 68 tag pages and the tag index. The census covers the complete build. Interactive and visual coverage uses representative content, with exhaustive checks for the 23 tables and ten diagrams.

The table records an observed result in each theme and on mobile. “Pass” means the specific observed presentation or interaction passed. “Shared” in the page column means the regular frame. The visual and accessibility columns describe the sample, including unresolved issues. A component with a known issue can still have an approved visual treatment. The rendered Markdown has no ordinary blockquotes outside callouts, footnotes, article H4–H6, task checkboxes, math, citation popups or article horizontal rules. The common separator in the page frame belongs to the frame. The templates have no featured-card or excerpt slot on folder listings. No published encrypted, Bases or Canvas page exercised those optional interfaces. Native Safari, physical mobile devices, spoken output and field battery measurements are outside the measured matrix. Redirects, fonts, content index, SEO, RSS, CNAME and sitemap were assessed as resources rather than interactive components.
